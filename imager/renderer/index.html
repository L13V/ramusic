The stick is ready

  1. Plug it into a USB port on the OPS module — the PC slot in the back of the panel, not a panel-front port.
  2. Power the panel on and open the firmware boot menu (usually F7, sometimes F12 or Esc) and pick the USB stick.
  3. To make it permanent, set the stick first in the boot order in BIOS setup (Del or F2).
  4. RAMTECH OS boots straight to the dashboard and shows a QR code — scan it with a phone to sign in to Spotify. That sign-in is remembered across reboots.
